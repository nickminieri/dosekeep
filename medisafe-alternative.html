What changed at Medisafe

Medisafe was the standard free medication reminder for a decade. That ended in two steps: in late 2024 the free plan was capped at 2 medications, and on January 1, 2026 a subscription (about $5/month or $40/year) became required for full use, with users outside the US hit hardest. If you take more than two medications, the app you relied on now has a monthly fee.

Full disclosure: I make DoseKeep, one of the alternatives below. I've tried to keep this comparison honest anyway, including the cases where another choice is better.

When something else is the better pick

Pick Medisafe if drug interaction checks and caregiver alerts matter more to you than the subscription cost. Those features are real, and DoseKeep deliberately does not offer medical guidance of any kind.

Pick MyTherapy if you want symptom and measurement journaling alongside meds and don't mind an account in the cloud.

Pick Apple Health Medications if you take one or two meds, want zero setup, and its single follow-up reminder is enough to catch you.

Pick DoseKeep if you want the thing Medisafe used to be: every feature free, unlimited medications, reminders that genuinely don't give up, the whole household (pets included) on one private app, and a history you can hand to your doctor. There is no account and no server, which also means no one can ever put your medication list behind a paywall or lock you out of it.

Switching takes about two minutes

There is no import to wait on: open DoseKeep, add your medications and times, and you're done. No sign-up, no email, no trial clock. If you ever leave, your history exports to CSV or PDF, free.
